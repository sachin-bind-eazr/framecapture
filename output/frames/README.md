# Joy of Giving frames

The ten named PNGs in this directory are finished 1080 x 1350 transparent overlays exported from the application's frame renderer. Every frame contains the original Witty logo at bottom left, the original The Good Box Project logo at bottom right, a small Joy of Giving heading, and the exact message between the logos.

Open `all-frames.png` to review the collection. The white centers in that contact sheet represent transparent photo openings in the individual PNGs. The `camera-*.png` and `captured-preview.png` files are browser verification screenshots using a simulated camera.

## Artwork and prompt set

Artwork was generated with the built-in image generation tool. The original Witty and The Good Box Project images were supplied as supporting logo references where applicable. Exact branding and typography are rendered from the original assets by `lib/frameComposer.ts`, rather than relying on generated lettering.

Shared generation brief: a portrait 4:5 children's illustrated event photo frame; thin decorative edge strips; an enormous genuinely alpha-transparent center; a small Joy of Giving heading; modest centered footer message; Witty at bottom left and The Good Box Project at bottom right; no photograph, mockup, white-filled opening, or checkerboard. Decorations remain near the outer edges. Final composition widens both side strips to 76px in the 1080px design space, adds small illustrated top corners, and reserves a 106px footer for original branding.

## Verification

All ten overlays were checked for 1080 x 1350 dimensions, fully transparent central photo area, and pixels from both original logos. Frame selection was exercised for every option. Camera controls were checked at 320 x 568, 390 x 844 and 1440 x 900. Simulated camera capture produced a 1080 x 1350 JPEG, and local image upload reached the framed preview successfully.
